Dr. Apurva Mittal is a Bengaluru-based psychiatrist with 9 years of experience and currently practises at Amaha Health. She completed her senior residency and postdoctoral fellowship in Geriatric Psychiatry at NIMHANS, Bengaluru, where she also worked as an Assistant Professor on research involving dementia, mild cognitive impairment, and non-invasive brain stimulation. Her clinical specialisations include anxiety and depressive disorders, bipolar disorder, OCD, schizophrenia, sleep disorders, adult ADHD, and geriatric mental health, with particular interests in women’s mental health, perinatal psychiatry, and dementia care. 
Beyond clinical practice, she contributes to mental health awareness, community initiatives, and policy-related work, with a focus on evidence-based and personalised patient care.

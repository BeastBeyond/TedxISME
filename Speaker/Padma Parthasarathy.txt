Padma Parthasarathy is an Independent Director and corporate leader with nearly four decades of experience in management, consulting, technology, sales, and strategy. An alumna of IIM Ahmedabad, she previously led the Global Consulting and Digital businesses at Tech Mahindra and played a key role in integrating Tech Mahindra and Mahindra Satyam in 2009.
She serves on the boards of TNS India Foundation and Action NorthEast Trust and advises institutions on digital enterprise and development management. From 2020 to 2026, she served on the Governing Council of the Confederation of Indian Industry’s (CII) Centre for Digital Transformation, promoting digital transformation in Indian businesses. She was featured on Forbes India's Women Power list of Self-Made Women in 2020 and the 75 Women in STEAM list in 2022.

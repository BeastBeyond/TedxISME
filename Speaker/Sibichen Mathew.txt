Dr. Sibichen Mathew is a former Director General in the Indian Revenue Service (IRS), with extensive experience in taxation, fraud investigation, white-collar crime, cybercrime, and corporate governance. He holds a PhD in Sociology/Public Finance, an LLB, and a postgraduate qualification from IIM Bangalore. He is an accomplished author and TEDx speaker, known for books such as You Just Got Cheated, When the Boss Is Wrong, and Making People Pay. His achievements include academic gold medals, government service recognition, and the Distinguished Toastmaster certification.
